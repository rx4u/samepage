## Plan check
The database plan check does not apply (no database). Its replacement runs in CI on `fixtures/perf`:
- `sp index` finishes under 5 s.
- Every `index/boot/{surface}.md` is under 8,000 characters.
- Every page file holds at most 20 rows (tag indexes at most 50) and every `next:` link resolves.
- `sp check` passes, then fails as expected on each of 12 broken-file fixtures in `fixtures/broken/`.
